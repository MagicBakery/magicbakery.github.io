Academic Tone

1. Educational Scale

1.1. Rank D: Advocacy

This ranking means that the content is biased, opinionated, and urges the viewer to engage to help win by framing a situation as a win-lose situation.

This rating does not mean the article has no educational, factual, or informative content, but such content was presented to help a specific side of a win-lose situation win.

1.2. Rank C: Neutral

This ranking means that the content is neutral, not adovacy, but falls short of being a public benefit.

This could happen when the content only benefits a specific private entity or the information is incorrect.

A simple shoutout or thank you note would fall into this category unless it was done as participation to provide insight to people's preferences in a public decision planning.

1.3. Rank B: Educational

This ranking means that the content is education per 501(c)(3).

1.4. Rank A: Engaging

This ranking means that the content is education per 501(c)(3) and engaging to the casual general audience even when they weren't seeking a lesson on the subject.

If the article uses a hook, the hook stills needs to satisfied the non-advocacy tone.

This means that the situation still needs to be presented fairly without creating an enemy.

1.5. Rank S: Bridging

This ranking means that the content is education per 501(c)(3) and it can convinced biased viewers to become neutral.
