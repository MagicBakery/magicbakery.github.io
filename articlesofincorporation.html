Quest Society Articles of Incorporation

1. ARTICLE I - Name

The name of this corporation shall be Quest Society Foundation.

2. ARTICLE II - Type of Corporation

Quest Society Foundation is designated as a public benefit corporation.

3. ARTICLE III - Purpose Clause

Organized and operated exclusively for charitable purposes, including education and civic improvement. Specific mission description: accomplishes purposes by identifying community needs, defining public-benefit "Quests," and administering a program that funds creation/delivery of educational and civic-improvement outputs, including open-source applications and publicly accessible materials.

4. ARTICLE IV - Registered Agent for Service of Process

The name and address in the State of California of this corporation's initial agent for service of process is: [Redacted for this evaluation version.]

5. ARTICLE V - Initial Registered Office / Principal Office Address

The initial street address of the corporation's principal office in the State of California is the same as the address of its initial agent for service of process set forth in Article IV.

6. ARTICLE VI - Limitations

Quest Society Foundation does not operate for the benefit of private interests. Its activities are conducted to further educational and civic-improvement objectives and to produce public-benefit outputs. No part of the net earnings of the corporation shall inure to the benefit of any private individual.

7. ARTICLE VII - Dissolution Clause

Upon the dissolution of this corporation, assets shall be distributed for one or more exempt purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

8. ARTICLE VIII - Membership Provisions

This corporation shall have no members within the meaning of California Corporations Code Section 5056. Any reference to "members" elsewhere in the corporation's governing documents refers to the participants in the corporation's programs and activities, and does not confer the statutory rights of membership under the California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law.

9. ARTICLE IX - Directors

The number of directors of this corporation shall not be less than three (3). The exact number of directors and any changes to that number shall be fixed in accordance with the corporation's Bylaws.

10. ARTICLE X - Incorporator Information

The name and address of the incorporator of this corporation is the same as the name and address of the initial agent for service of process set forth in Article IV.

11. ARTICLE XI - Effective Date

These Articles of Incorporation shall become effective upon filing with the California Secretary of State.
